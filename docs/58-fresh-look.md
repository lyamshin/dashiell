# A fresh look, 2026-10-18

*The designer asked for a once-over of everything before pushing on. His last word on the writing: "it's improving. It's vaguely playable now, which it wasn't before. But it's so dense and unclear." This reads two current nights end to end (`npm run read -- --seed 7 --tier 0 --type murder --no-choices` and `--seed 7 --tier 4 --no-choices`) against his nine playthrough notes, and checks the plans against what's actually on the page.*

## What's working

- **The puzzle.** Small board, whole accounts, watchers, two broken stories at one hour, means with lead time, the breaking line in the report. Four rounds of blind testers solved by deduction, the designer solved his night, and nobody calls it opaque. Leave it alone.
- **The architecture.** Bones (propositions) → composition → plain realization → voicings → texture, with a round trip that proves every fact survives. It's the right shape, and every remaining problem below is fixable inside it.
- **Short, concrete jokes about objects and trades.** "It belongs in the hall-stand drawer at Mrs. Nagy's, and the whole block knows it, because the landlady shows it to anybody who's late with the rent." That's the voice.

## What "dense" actually is now

It isn't the voice any more, and it isn't page length. Reading the two nights, nearly every heavy passage is one of these:

1. **The same fact, two or three times on a page.** Medium 7's search: "everybody who drops in at the Delmonico rooms knows the spare is in the sugar tin. The key that let them in was the spare, and the spare lived in the sugar tin at the Delmonico rooms." The sugar tin is said four times in two pages. Raw 7 says Bledsoe's evening is "still a blank" on two pages running. The find, the thought and the hand-off each restate the fact, so the eye can't tell new from old.
2. **Recitation by hour.** Watchers: "Eight o'clock, Mosley, Bledsoe and Hanrahan. That was everybody. Then nine: Mosley, Rafferty and Hanrahan, and that was the whole place. At ten I had Mosley and Hanrahan, and not another soul." A person says: "Mosley and Hanrahan were here all evening. Miss Bledsoe ate at eight and left. Mr. Rafferty came in at nine, like every night, and was gone by ten. Nobody else." Same facts, told by what changed. Accounts do it too ("Hour by hour, Hargrove was with me at eight; Steinbach was with me at nine; …").
3. **The thought restates instead of concluding.** After Mrs. Nagy's hours, the thought says them again. It should say what they mean: "So Bledsoe had been in the house at nine, where the revolver was kept, and gone before ten."
4. **Continuity slips.** Sitting down twice, arriving twice ("It was no distance to Moskowitz's…" then "I went down to Moskowitz's…"), asking a seated landlady to sit, the client already at the scene with no reason.
5. **The office out of order.** Names dropped with no gloss ("by Mosley, who'd come round to collect a debt"), the neighbour's key mentioned before the neighbour, money in the drawer before the client pays, the cause split from the finding.
6. **Tics.** The same simile frame twice a night ("as if the tables could help", "as if the house might want to add something"), a gag three times (the radiator), "as it turned out".
7. **Assembly bugs.** "I asked where the dead man would spent the evening." "I don't like gossip, but He threw her out of a lease." "Why: He threw them out of a lease."

All of the designer's nine notes fall into these seven.

## The measurement problem

Our blind reads have asked LLM readers which page they prefer, and they have preferred the fuller, more voiced page every time (80–85%), while the designer keeps saying "too dense." The readers aren't wrong about voice; they're the wrong test for density. From here the gate is a **skim test**: a reader reads a page once, answers three factual questions drawn from its propositions, and quotes any sentence they had to read twice. Fewer rereads and the same or better accuracy is a pass. Preference stays as a secondary check.

## The plan, in order

1. **Clarity build** (now): said-once (a fact is realized once per page, the givens once per night outside the turn and report); watchers and accounts told by change; the thought gives the consequence; continuity; the office in a natural order with every name glossed; tic caps; assembly lints; the poison timing; all nine notes. Gated by the skim test.
2. **Voice kit** (now, in parallel; it doesn't touch the game): the recording script and the recorder page with stop and resume.
3. **Then, the designer's read.** The next real signal is him playing a night, not another measure.
