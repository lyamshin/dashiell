## Page 1 — the office, midnight

> Midnight. My office was a room at the top of a walk-up on Mulberry Street, and the radiator had decided that tonight it would be a sculpture. Frost had got in on the inside of the window.
>
> Ilse Hauck came up the stairs with new gloves and the price tag still tucked in one cuff. "My sister was married to Isidore Sirkin," she said. "He's dead."
>
> Sirkin was a buildings inspector. He could close a building with a signature, and had closed two. Crowninshield, the dentist, found him in his walk-up over the drugstore on Grand Street at half past eleven. The police called it a fall.
>
> "The coroner doesn't," Hauck said. "Chloral in a glass of rye. That's a sleeping drug. He says Sirkin drank it between nine and ten, and never woke up."
>
> "Who'd want him asleep that long?"
>
> "Start with Steinbach, the piano teacher. Sirkin closed his school. Steinbach said so to anyone who'd listen, and some who wouldn't." She put a hundred dollars on the desk. "Find out who. Then keep it quiet."
>
> I took the money. The radiator ticked once, like it wanted a cut.
