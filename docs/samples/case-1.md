# Hotel Saint Clair — case 1

**Seed** 1 · **Attempts** 8 · **Detective** Humphrey

## 1. The Truth

Antonio Marchetti, a photographer for the rotogravure section, the victim's rival in trade, killed Maureen Quill, a society columnist, with strangling with a cord on the Service Stairs at 10:30 PM. Antonio Marchetti needed the victim silent (silence-a-witness). Antonio Marchetti had been in the Kitchen earlier in the evening, before 10:30 PM and was alone with Maureen Quill when it happened.

- **Antonio Marchetti** (murder): Antonio Marchetti is alone with Maureen Quill in the Service Stairs from 10:00 PM to 10:30 PM, and kills Maureen Quill at 10:30 PM.
- **Willa Prentiss** (fence): Willa Prentiss hands a parcel of stolen goods to a man at the Kitchen at 10:30 PM.
- **Delia Mulcahy** (gambling-debt): Delia Mulcahy slips out to the Street from 6:30 PM to 7:00 PM to settle with a bookmaker.
- **Marion Winslow** (forged-identity): Marion Winslow is not the person the register says. Nothing is hidden about the evening; the lie is in the paperwork.
- **Lorraine Ashby** (embezzling): Lorraine Ashby goes through the papers in the Victim's Suite from 8:00 PM while the victim is downstairs.
- **Beatrice Ellery** (secret-drinking): Beatrice Ellery drinks alone in the Bar from 10:00 PM to 10:30 PM and will claim to have been anywhere else.

- Delia Mulcahy was not on the Service Stairs at 10:30 PM, on two independent sources. _(c045, c063, c103, c121)_

- Beatrice Ellery was not on the Service Stairs at 10:30 PM, on two independent sources. _(c039, c054, c073, c114, c132)_

**Motive.** silence-a-witness, on two independent sources. _(c162, c163)_

## 8. Red herrings

**Innocents who lie about the murder tick:**

- Willa Prentiss claims the Bar at 10:30 PM and was really in the Kitchen. Reason: Willa Prentiss hands a parcel of stolen goods to a man at the Kitchen at 10:30 PM.
- Beatrice Ellery claims the Lobby at 10:30 PM and was really in the Bar. Reason: Beatrice Ellery drinks alone in the Bar from 10:00 PM to 10:30 PM and will claim to have been anywhere else.

**Innocents with a motive:**

- Willa Prentiss — debt: owed the victim money.
- Lorraine Ashby — jealousy: jealous of the victim.
