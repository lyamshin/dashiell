**Seed** 4 · **Attempts** 6 · **Detective** Humphrey

## 1. The Truth

Wilhelm Hochstetter, the night manager, the victim's landlord, killed Salvatore Alfano, a retired dry-goods wholesaler, with a push from the roof garden in the Roof Garden at 9:30 PM. Wilhelm Hochstetter stands to inherit (inheritance). Wilhelm Hochstetter had been at the Front Desk earlier in the evening, before 9:30 PM and was alone with Salvatore Alfano when it happened.
