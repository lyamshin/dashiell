# The Wexford Arms — case 3

**Seed** 3 · **Attempts** 1 · **Detective** Humphrey

## 1. The Truth

Adelaide Havemeyer, a piano teacher, the victim's rival in trade, killed Patrick Sweeney, a shipping clerk who had come into money, with a push from the roof garden in the Roof Garden at 10:00 PM. Adelaide Havemeyer was about to be exposed by the victim (exposure). Adelaide Havemeyer had been at the Front Desk earlier in the evening, before 10:00 PM and was alone with Patrick Sweeney when it happened.
