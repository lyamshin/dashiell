# The Ardsley — case 8

**Seed** 8 · **Attempts** 1 · **Detective** Humphrey

## 1. The Truth

Agnes Sweeney, a photographer for the rotogravure section, named in the victim's will, killed Ernst Obermann, a bootlegger with the lease on the top floor, with a push from the roof garden in the Roof Garden at 9:30 PM. Agnes Sweeney needed the victim silent (silence-a-witness). Agnes Sweeney had been at the Front Desk earlier in the evening, before 9:30 PM and was alone with Ernst Obermann when it happened.

- Carmela Ruggiero was not in the Roof Garden at 9:30 PM, on two independent sources. _(c034, c049, c086, c104)_
