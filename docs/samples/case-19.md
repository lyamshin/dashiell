# The Tarleton — case 19

**Seed** 19 · **Attempts** 1 · **Detective** Humphrey

## 1. The Truth

Concetta Vitale, a chorus girl between engagements, the victim's business partner, killed Emilio Moretti, the owner of the hotel, with a gunshot in the Roof Garden at 9:00 PM. Concetta Vitale needed the victim silent (silence-a-witness). Concetta Vitale had been at the Front Desk earlier in the evening, before 9:00 PM and was alone with Emilio Moretti when it happened.
