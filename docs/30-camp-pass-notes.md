## The read-through, and what it trimmed

Three helpers took the decks (the character deck with grounding and tail; the arrival and search decks; the decks around the facts), and I took hiring, office and entrances. Then I read seed 3 at Medium (`--tier 4`) and seed 11 at Raw start to finish as a player against the camp golden, and seed 7 at Hard-boiled for its night pages. Where two or three jokes stacked in one paragraph or one short page, I took some back out. Fourteen came out of the first read:

- **Seed 3, page 1.** "…learned to count their fingers after" (the broker's street line) sat two sentences above the hiring's "kept two fingers on it". The street line went back to "count their change".
- **Seed 3, pages 2 and 3.** The scene and the first search had a "which" joke in every paragraph: "which is more than my own room ever gets", "which made the climb no shorter", "which is what I call it when I have nothing" and "one good chair, which I envied". The last one also echoed the office's "good chair". The establish and search-act turns came out.
- **Seed 3, page 4.** The landlady had three jokes in five lines (the cold, the coins she trusted none of, and "fooled nobody"). The watch line lost its turn.
- **Seed 3, page 8.** The speakeasy arrival had four. Crowninshield's activity ("with the look of someone who had been stood up before") went back to "waiting, and not for me", because the closing observation prints the activity a second time (see below).
- **Seed 11, page 3.** Donnelly's look got an extra line ("I stayed the right way up.") on a page that already had three. That line came out. So did "and not well" after a drunk singing on the block, since the case's own anchor that night is a drunk singing under the window.
- **Seed 7, page 4.** "which is my method, such as it is" came right after "which is what I call it when I have nothing". It came out.

Then I counted the two tics that could stack on a night page: a ", which …" tag on the end of a sentence (62 new ones) and the detective's feet (18). I took out 26 more, all in the decks that open or pad nearly every night page: errand, carry, hours, answer, bridge, thought, search-act, establish, return and one activity card. What's left is about one feet joke per deck, not one per page.

## Page one has no room to grow

The first full suite failed one test: `m5-engine`, "carries every sentence of the briefing", on seed 10. Page one had lost its whole entrance ("Vitale came up the stairs slow…"). Page one has a 440-word ceiling (`OPENING_CEILING`). Most office pages already run within twenty words of it (median 425 on `main`), and when a page goes over, the ceiling cuts the entrance first. The pass had made the office, entrance and hiring cards a few words longer each: the knuckle, the radiator, the closings. So over 180 first pages (seeds 1–60 × difficulties 1–3), the entrance went missing on 14 pages, against 4 on `main`.

So every office, entrance and hiring card was cut back to about its old length. The joke stays and the setup gets shorter ("{name} came in wearing mink, and neither chair was quite up to her."). The seed-3 hiring kept the golden's "kept two fingers on it until I picked it up. I respected that." and dropped its "I asked what the trouble was worth." After that, the entrance is missing on the same 4 pages as `main` and no others. **Rule for later passes: a card on page one may not grow.** Every other page has the room.

## Constraints the helpers found

- **activity:** a card is one sentence, `{name} was <doing>.` The engine puts the hour at the first comma and builds "stopped <doing> and looked up" from the part before it, so the turn goes after the comma.
- **The engine adds "X was Y." after a person's first mention** in an errand or carry line, so a joke placed right after a `{who}`/`{name}` sentence gets split from it. Those jokes were moved.
- **followup:** cards with `ask: rest` must keep "rest of the evening", "rest of the night" or "other hours" (test/m10-testimony.test.ts).
- **character:** a look or street card that opens on a past-tense verb is skipped by `isSubjectless`. Look cards stay one or two sentences, because first sight is three to five sentences in all.
- **place-ambient** may not carry a figure at all, and **search-act** may not say whether anything was or wasn't there.
- **The overlap check** flags common five-word runs too ("of a man who had", "as if it were the", "I'd have done the same"). Several good lines were reworded for that reason alone.

## Not fixed

- **The arrival's closing observation prints the person's activity a second time,** so an activity with a turn after the comma lands twice on one page ("…keeping time for nobody." / "…past four in the morning, keeping time for nobody."). This is being fixed on `m12-conversation`. Until then the activity turns are kept modest.
- **Twelve story lines share a five-word run with the corpus** (STY-089, 121, 135, 168, 171, 183, 193, 233, 248, 328, 329, 331). They were there before this pass and they state facts, so I left them alone. A helper drafted clean rewrites; they need a check that no fact changes.
- **The ", which …" tag is the house tic now.** The golden uses it ("which tells you about the other one", "which in her language was a warm welcome"), and it works one at a time. Future cards should reach for other shapes.
